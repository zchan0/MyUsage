LiteLLM provider icon

ProviderIcon-litellm.svg is a simplified monochrome drawing of the high-speed
train used by LiteLLM. It preserves the train motif, with new vector geometry
and reduced detail for 18 pt macOS menu-bar rendering. It is not an official
LiteLLM SVG asset and does not include the LiteLLM wordmark.

Reference: https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/blob/main/ui/litellm-dashboard/public/assets/logos/litellm_logo.jpg
Official website: https://www.litellm.ai/
